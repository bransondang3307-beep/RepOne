## Objective
Build a rep detector that tracks a complete movement cycle, so that pauses and renewed acceleration never count as
new reps. The owner's requirement is that moving up, stopping halfway and continuing up is one movement. The task
was to audit and reproduce before redesigning.

## Current detector findings (0.1.0 on `main`)
- **What it measures.** It integrates the bias-corrected gyro rate about the calibrated axis into an angle, and uses
  `|angle|` as excursion. The accelerometer is unused. It assumes rotation.
- **State machine.** SYNCING → IDLE at stillness (low-pass of the rate < 0.05 rad/s for 300 ms). IDLE → MOVING at
  20 % of ROM. MOVING → rep at 10 %: `full` if the peak was ≥ 80 %, else `partial`.
- **Owner's paused-upward case: it already passed on 0.1.0.** The design is position-based, so a pause never ended
  the cycle. That behaviour is preserved.
- **Defects reproduced** with a frozen 0.1.0 copy (ADR 0009 §1.3):
  - A 15 s mid-cycle pause gave **3 phantom partials and 0 full**: the 10 s timeout reset, re-zeroed at the paused
    position and ran out of phase.
  - A 20 s hold at the top mistimed the next rep and missed one.
  - A disconnect during a hold made later reps fire about 1 s early. It re-synced at the top, assuming that was the
    start.
  - Holding with small jitter meant it **never synced** (0 reps).
  - Incomplete or interrupted cycles were silent.
- **Evidence.** Synthetic only. No physical evidence exists for either version.

## What changed
- **`rotational-rep-fsm-0.2.0`** (`packages/rep-engine/src/RotationalRepDetector.ts`):
  - **Cycle completion.** A cycle ends only by returning to within 10 % of start. Pauses, slowdowns, reversals above
    the band and re-acceleration all continue the same cycle.
  - **Long cycles** (> 10 s) stay open and are flagged `long_cycle`. Nothing is abandoned or promoted.
  - **Settled** means the angle stayed within a 2 % ROM band for 300 ms. This position criterion tolerates jitter and
    is never true at constant speed.
  - **Gravity at rest** (when the rotation axis is not vertical) gives an absolute, unsigned excursion. It is used to
    correct drift during mid-cycle pauses (only if it agrees within 20 % ROM; otherwise the cycle is flagged
    `gravity_disagreement`) and to re-anchor after a gap.
  - **Unfinished outcomes.** Cycles that end without a rep are reported by `takeUnfinishedCycles()` / `finish()` as
    `interrupted` (gap) or `incomplete` (session end). They are never counted.
  - Config: `restBandFraction` and `minGravityPlaneFraction` are new; `maxRepDurationMs` is now `longCycleMs`; the
    rate low-pass parameters are removed.
- **Offline evaluation** (`src/trialEvaluation.ts`, `scripts/evaluate-trials.cjs`, `npm run evaluate:trials`):
  - `rep-trial-v1` trial files hold `RawSensorSample[]` plus labels.
  - Metrics: full precision and recall, missed reps, extra detections, false counts during holds, events during
    pauses, full/partial errors, signed completion timing, and unfinished cycles.
  - Synthetic and sensor data, and tuning and evaluation splits, are always reported separately.
  - `--detector legacy` compares against 0.1.0. `evaluateReps` additionally returns its matched pairs.
- **Regression fixtures** (`test/scenarios/`, all SYNTHETIC):
  - a trajectory generator with pauses, tremor, constant speed, dropouts, irregular timing, rates, axis and
    mount-flip;
  - 19 scenarios with ground-truth timing;
  - a frozen 0.1.0 reference.
- **Docs:**
  - ADR 0009 (Proposed);
  - bench capture and labelling protocol;
  - a pointer in ADR 0002;
  - the RO-032 task row and section.

## Known limitations / risks
- **All evidence is synthetic.** The tremor model, noise levels and the gravity model are assumptions. Real MPU-6050
  vibration and mounting effects are unmeasured.
- **Touch-and-go:** a reversal above the 10 % band merges two reps (ADR 0009 §8 C).
- **Disconnects:** a sample gap loses that cycle. Without gravity (vertical axis), later reps can shift by half a
  cycle; they are flagged `reference_assumed`.
- **Session start:** the session must begin at the start position.
- **Long cycles** (> 10 s) count if they really completed. Option B1 would abandon them instead.
- **Translation** (weight stack) is not supported. Option D proposes a separate zero-velocity-bounded detector.
- **The app does not yet** call `finish()` or read unfinished cycles. It is unaffected otherwise, and
  `algorithm_version` changes to 0.2.0.
