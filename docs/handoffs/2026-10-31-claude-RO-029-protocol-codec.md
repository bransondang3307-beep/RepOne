## Objective
This is part 1 of the owner's real-sensor pipeline:
- specify a versioned wire protocol for MPU-6050 data over BLE (and USB for the Uno R3);
- build a transport-independent encoder and decoder, with golden fixtures shared with firmware;
- test it without a phone, a radio or a sensor.

## What changed
- **ADR 0008 (Proposed)** and **`docs/protocols/imu-stream-v1.md`**:
  - versioning and brand-neutral GATT UUIDs;
  - raw int16 counts plus full-scale codes on the wire, converted at the transport boundary;
  - axis order and frame, with no remapping;
  - the u32 device clock and its unwrapping; u16 sequence numbers and loss detection;
  - stream ids, resets and stale-data rules;
  - source identity from the board serial;
  - framing and CRC-16 validation;
  - the MTU table, batching, and sample-rate and bandwidth calculations;
  - status codes and their mapping to ADR 0007 errors; reconnection;
  - the USB hex-line representation;
  - the capabilities the unselected wireless board needs.
  - Inherited decisions are marked as such.
- **`@repone/sensor-protocol`** (a new workspace package that depends only on `@repone/sensor-contracts`):
  - `decodePacket` / `encodeSamplePacket` / `encodeDeviceInfo`;
  - `crc16CcittFalse`;
  - unit conversion (`accelRawToMS2`, `gyroRawToRadS`; MPU-6050 register-map sensitivities);
  - `maxSamplesForMtu`;
  - `StreamTracker`, which turns packets into `RawSensorSample`s with source identity, `sample_gap` and `saturated`
    flags, and stream, stale, duplicate and restart events;
  - `parseSerialLine` for USB lines.
- **Golden fixtures** (`fixtures/v1/golden-packets.json`, labelled **synthetic**): 8 valid and 17 invalid packets,
  plus conversion checks.
  - They were generated by an independent Python implementation of the spec. The TypeScript codec reproduces every
    byte, and the firmware encoder (part 2) must too.
- **`docs/hardware-protocol.md` §2–3:**
  - point to the spec;
  - the draft CSV and its `# repone h0 v1` header are superseded;
  - the open questions are updated.
- **Other docs:** `docs/architecture.md` (module and boundary rows) and the `README.md` layout.
- **`scripts/check-boundaries.mjs`:** the new package is scanned and allowed to depend only on `sensor-contracts`.
- **`package-lock.json`:** the new workspace link only (18 lines).

The tests cover:
- valid decoding and explicit unit conversion;
- truncated (every prefix of every golden packet), malformed, corrupt (every single-bit flip), unsupported-version
  and reserved-bit packets;
- invalid numeric values: NaN, infinity, fractions, out of int16, bad range codes;
- sequence gaps, duplicates, and sequence and u32 clock wrap;
- time going backwards;
- multiple samples per notification;
- stream start, restart and reset (reconnect), and stale packets;
- payload size and MTU limits;
- per-channel independence;
- preservation of source identity, and no `synthetic` flag on decoded real-device data.

**Not tested here:** any radio, phone or sensor. These are codec tests on synthetic bytes.

## Decisions / ADRs
- **ADR 0008, Proposed.** Open questions for the owner: the default rate (50 Hz proposed) and the wireless board. The
  owner accepts the ADR explicitly.

## Known limitations / risks
- **MPU-6050 sensitivities are nominal;** factory tolerance isn't corrected. Calibration policy is unchanged.
- **The ~185-byte iPhone MTU is an assumption,** to be measured on the real board (spec §9).
- **The `invalid_data` policy** (how many rejects fail a run) is settled in part 3's adapter.
- **RO-016 overlap.** This proposal overlaps RO-016's scope; the owner or ChatGPT should re-scope it.
- **`TASKS.md` is untouched.** The RO-029 row is added by PR #23, which isn't merged.
